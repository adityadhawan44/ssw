Your privacy,
explained plainly.

This notice describes the information this publication uses to provide its current reader and editorial features.

Information we use

If you create a reader account, Supabase processes the email address and authentication information needed to sign in. If you choose Google sign-in, Google also handles that sign-in flow. We do not receive or store your password as readable text.

When you use reader features, the site may store bookmarks, likes, comments and a display name in the publication database. Comments are reviewed; comments approved for display and the display name associated with them may be visible to other readers. Do not put private case facts or sensitive personal information in a comment.

Reading preferences and items saved while signed out may be stored in this browser using local storage. These device-only settings are not synchronized unless the feature specifically says they are.

Editorial enquiries

If you submit the contact form, we use your name, email address, request category and message to review and respond to that enquiry. Do not use the form to send confidential legal documents or urgent requests. Enquiry records are visible only to the publication owner and are retained while needed to handle the request and maintain an editorial record.

Service providers and security

The public site is hosted by Vercel. Authentication and publication data are provided by Supabase. These services process information as needed to operate the site under their own terms and privacy notices. We do not currently use advertising trackers or a third-party analytics product on this site. Your browser may make ordinary technical requests needed to load the pages and assets.

Newsletter

Newsletter subscriptions are not currently open, and this site does not collect email addresses through a newsletter signup form.

Your choices and requests

You can change reading preferences in the site controls and remove browser-only saved items from your device. For a question about an account or a request to review or remove information, use Contact & corrections and choose “Privacy or account-data request.” We may need to verify account ownership before acting on an account request.

Changes to this notice

This notice may change as features are added. The date above shows when it was last updated. This is a plain-language description of this website’s current practices, not legal advice.
