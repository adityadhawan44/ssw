A clear guide to the purpose and limits of this publication.

General legal information

SHWETA publishes general educational material about law, rights, cases and related ideas. It is not legal advice, does not assess an individual matter and does not create a lawyer–client relationship. Laws and procedures can change, and their application depends on the facts. Check current primary sources and speak with a qualified professional about a specific situation.

Sources and editorial work

We aim to identify sources and distinguish legal materials from commentary. A summary is not a substitute for the full judgment, statute or official guidance. If you identify a potential error, please send it through Contact & corrections.

Accounts and reader contributions

Keep your account access secure. Do not submit confidential or sensitive personal information in comments or editorial enquiries. Comments may be moderated and may be removed if they expose private information, contain abuse, or do not fit the discussion. You remain responsible for material you submit.

Content and links

Unless a page says otherwise, publication text and design are provided for reading and personal reference. Do not republish substantial material without permission. Links to external sources are provided for convenience; SHWETA does not control those sites or guarantee their availability or content.

Availability

We work to keep the site available and correct but cannot guarantee uninterrupted operation or that every item is current. Features may change as the publication develops. The site should not be used for emergencies, deadlines or urgent legal action.

Contact

For editorial, correction, privacy or account questions, use the editorial contact form.
